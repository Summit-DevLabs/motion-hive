Our Process: From Sketch to Screen

1\. Concept & Previs

It starts with vision. We work closely with directors and producers to understand the story and develop initial concepts, storyboards, and previsualizations that set the foundation.

2\. Asset Creation

Next, we build digital environments, props, and characters from the ground up using industry-standard tools like Maya, Houdini, and ZBrush—then texture and rig them for animation.

3\. Simulation & Animation

Want that car to flip through the air and explode mid-spin? We’ve got it. Our animators and FX artists create dynamic movement and physics-driven simulations that are both believable and thrilling.

4\. Compositing & Polish

This is where it all comes together. We blend VFX elements into live-action plates, fine-tune lighting, color, and depth, and ensure the final shot feels authentic and cinematic.
